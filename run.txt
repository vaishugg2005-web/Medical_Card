B. LOCAL MULTI-DATABASE ARCHITECTURE & REALTIME SYNC:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Medora persists all domain data into dedicated SQLite files located directly in .\backend:
  1. patient.db: Stores patients, emergency_contacts, allergies, medications,
     medical_conditions, surgeries, visits, prescriptions, qr_tokens, consents, consultations.
  2. doctor.db: Stores doctors, hospitals, doctor_verifications, doctor_documents.
  3. admin.db: Stores users, audit_logs, emergency_access_events, notifications, access_requests.
  4. Real-time Sync: MultiDB listener (SQLAlchemy after_commit hook) mirrors every transaction
     across the respective database files instantly and deterministically.

G. UPDATED MEDORA ARCHITECTURE & 3-AREA WORKSPACES:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Full-Height 3-Area Doctor Dashboard:

   • Editable Clinical Records:

2. Comprehensive 18-Workspace Patient Portal (Patient Dashboard):
   • Left Categorized Navigation (18 Workspaces):

       3. My Medical History      - Chronological medical timeline with multi-category filters
       4. Conditions & Diseases   - Active and historical diagnosed conditions
       5. Allergies & Warnings    - Severe allergies, anaphylaxis risks, clinical warnings
       6. Medications & Rx        - Active prescriptions, dosages, informational adherence reminders
       7. Surgeries & Procedures  - Surgical history, hospital, dates, clinical notes
       8. Consultations & Reports - Clinical visits, document library, report upload/download/deletion
       9. Appointments Queue      - Upcoming and past booked consultations with status badges
      10. Find a Doctor           - Real-time directory of verified doctors with search and booking modal
      11. My Requests             - Patient-initiated consultation and records requests
      12. Doctor Access Requests  - Pending clinical access requests from doctors with Approve/Reject
      13. Consent & Privacy       - Active consent grants with one-click revocation
      14. Emergency Medical Card  - QR card preview with browser-side jsPDF download
      15. Access History & Audits - Comprehensive tamper-evident access log, break-glass events
      16. Notifications Center    - Real-time alerts, mark as read, bulk mark all as read

      18. Help & Support          - Emergency guidance, FAQ knowledge base, support contact
   • Strict Privacy & Authorship Boundary:
       Patients can update their own permitted personal details (phone, address, instructions),
       while clinician-authored records (diagnoses, allergies, medications, surgeries, prescriptions)
       can only be authored or modified by verified attending doctors.
   • Ground-Truth AI Clinical Summary:
       Zero-hallucination deterministic synthesis generated solely from active database records.

3. Doctor Verification, Admin Approval & Patient Appointment Workflow:
   • Doctor Registration:
     Collects legal name, medical license, registration authority (NMC/State Council),
     jurisdiction, qualification, specialization, hospital, experience, and terms acceptance.
     Initial status is strictly PENDING with an audit reference (DOC-APP-2026-XXXX).
   • Admin Verification Center (Admin Portal):
     Real database-backed counts for Pending, Under Review, Verified, Rejected, Suspended.
     Searchable queue by doctor name and license; filters by status and specialization.
     Decision actions: Approve, Reject (mandatory reason), Request Information, Suspend, Reinstate.
   • Patient Portal Verified Doctor Directory (Appointments → Find a Doctor):
     Strict security barrier: ONLY doctors with verification_status == 'VERIFIED' and active accounts
     appear in the directory. Never displays pending, rejected, or suspended doctors.
     Empty State: If no doctors are available, renders:
       "No doctors are currently available for consultation. Please check again later."
       "Medora displays only doctors who have completed professional verification and are
       currently eligible to accept consultations. No matching doctors are available at the moment."
   • Appointment Booking & Doctor Queue:
     Patient selects verified doctor, consultation mode (In-Person OPD / Tele-Consultation),
     preferred date, slot time, and symptoms.
     Backend revalidates doctor verification and prevents slot collisions (409 Conflict).
     Doctor views appointment in queue and accepts (CONFIRMED) or declines (REJECTED).
   • Zero-Trust Privacy Principle:
     Appointment booking NEVER grants medical history access. Outpatient record access still
     strictly requires explicit Level 2 Patient Consent or Level 3 Emergency Break-Glass.

4. Cross-Portal Real-Time Synchronization Workflows:
   • Patient submits consultation request → Doctor's queue updates instantly.
   • Doctor requests outpatient consent → Patient receives approval prompt.
   • Patient approves or revokes consent → Doctor's authorization status updates immediately.
   • Doctor authors new clinical diagnosis or Rx → Patient timeline refreshes with notification.
   • Break-glass emergency override initiated → 15-minute countdown starts with audit logging.

J. TROUBLESHOOTING COMMON ISSUES:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Port 8000 or 5173 already in use:
   Stop existing process or run on another port:
   .\backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m uvicorn app.main:app --reload --port 8080

2. Python ModuleNotFoundError: 'app':
   Ensure PYTHONPATH is set before starting uvicorn:
   $env:PYTHONPATH="backend"

3. Execution Policy restrictions in PowerShell:
   Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process -ExecutionPolicy Bypass
